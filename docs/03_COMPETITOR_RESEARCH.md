## 0. The market shape (mid-2026)

Three layers of competition:
1. **The funded AI-visibility unicorn** — Profound. Owns measurement + content, now claims autonomy. This is the ceiling competitor we cannot out-fund-raise.
2. **The SEO incumbents** — Ahrefs, Semrush (plus their own AI-visibility bolt-ons). Own distribution and existing customer accounts; will add AI-visibility features as a line item, not a thesis.
3. **The AI-visibility startup wave** — Airix, AEO Engine, RankPrompt, SEO Stack, Indexsy, LocalRank, and the long tail. Surgical feature-point plays; none have shipped the closed loop.
4. **The existential threat** — the AI surfaces themselves (OpenAI, Google/DeepMind, Anthropic, Perplexity). They could ship a brand-authoring portal and commoditize the Brand-Truth layer.

---

## 1. Profound — the funded leader

**What they own:** Measurement + content + "autonomous marketing agents" (launched, named). A $96M Series C at $1B valuation (Feb 2026) means they out-spend us on engineering, distribution, compliance, and design. They are the reference point buyers will name first. They are *pixel-native* — their locus of action is the customer's marketing stack (ad pixels, content ops, marketing channels). They have the design polish we cannot match at MVP.

**What they miss (our open seam):**
- **Rep-native action, not repo-native.** Profound operates in the marketing stack; an engineering team merging a PR is not their natural buyer or locus. The technical-brand founder who can merge a schema.org JSON-LD PR but distrusts another marketing pixel is underserved.
- **Honest calibrated confidence intervals.** Funded leaders optimize the headline number; "we tell you precisely how much we don't know" (the Candor Report with CIs + coverage diagnostics + degradation alerts) is a differentiator they structurally cannot claim without re-architecting their causal story.
- **The aligned causal corpus across heterogeneous tenants.** Their measurement is strong *within* a tenant; the federated cross-tenant learning on an aligned coordinate system is the compounding moat they have not articulated.
- **Trust-first autonomy.** "Autonomous marketing agents" funded by a $1B raise is a marketing claim that overpromises; our autonomy dial earns escalation on demonstrated calibration + approval rate + overlap support and demotes automatically — a structurally different posture.

**How we co-exist and win:** We do not compete head-on. We wedge the *engineering-shipped, trust-first* audience the funded leader doesn't naturally reach; we bank the corpus they can't backfill; we win honesty- and trust-willing buyers who distrust "autonomous" marketing-ops claims. The deep claim from the intelligence core: a clone (and a funded leader who has *not* built the closed-loop causal corpus) can draft plausible recommendations but cannot *rank* them by expected uplift or give honest CIs — it's a horoscope. We sell the calibrated predictor, not the dashboard.

## 2. Ahrefs & Semrush — the SEO incumbents

**What they own:** SEO-distribution monopoly, existing customer accounts (millions of paying users), brand authority, crawl infrastructure, the keyword-ranking mental model that every SEO buyer already has. They have *added or are adding* AI-visibility features as a tab/line-item.

**What they miss:**
- **Native closed-loop AI visibility.** Their core is keyword-ranking telemetry bolted with an AI-visibility tab; the *intervention-outcome closed loop* (probe → brand card → schema PR → measure → lift) is not their architecture.
- **Repo-native action.** They deploy pixels and dashboards; they do not ship PRs into a customer's engineering stack.
- **A defensible causal corpus for AI surfaces.** Their data assets are crawl-and-keyword; the AI-surface consented panel + causal corpus is a different asset class.

**How we co-exist and win:** They win the SEO buyer who wants "and here's your AI score too." We win the buyer who wants the AI-visibility *result* (showing up in ChatGPT) shipped as an engineering action with proof. Their account-base advantage is real and durable; our wedge advantage is the result + the corpus. They are a *distribution* threat more than a *product* threat in Horizon 1.

## 3. The AI-visibility startup wave

Each is a surgical feature-point play. None has shipped the closed loop; none has the corpus moat. They are competitive in naming/share-of-voice but not in the intelligence thesis.

### Airix
- **What they own:** Multi-surface answer tracking; a polished "what does AI say about you" report.
- **What they miss:** The closed loop (probe → action → measurement); a causal story; Brand Truth as a primary source.
- **Open seam:** the action + the proof.

### AEO Engine
- **What they own:** Answer-engine-optimization tooling; content optimization for AI answers.
- **What they miss:** Honest measurement, the mult-tenant corpus, autonomy.
- **Open seam:** measurement with reported variance + the federated learning.

### RankPrompt
- **What they own:** Prompt-position / ranking-style tracking on AI answers.
- **What they miss:** Interventions (they track, they don't act); the brand-truth SOT.
- **Open seam:** acting on the tracking + proving the lift.

### SEO Stack
- **What they own:** A bundled SEO + AI-visibility checklist dashboard.
- **What they miss:** The closed loop, the corpus, the autonomy dial.
- **Open seam:** the calibrated predictor vs a checklist.

### Indexsy
- **What they own:** Indexability / crawlability focus for AI surfaces.
- **What they miss:** Visibility measurement across the answer layer (not just crawl).
- **Open seam:** answer-layer probing + the action.

### LocalRank
- **What they own:** Local-SMB AI visibility (the plumber/dentist segment).
- **What they miss:** The engineering-shipped locus (local SMBs cannot merge a PR — they need a different action surface, which is why we explicitly exclude them from the MVP ICP).
- **Open seam:** the local SMB is a *different product*; we don't pursue it until the agency tier can serve it indirectly.

### The long tail of newer AI-visibility startups
- **What they own:** Early-mover naming, a feature or two.
- **What they miss:** Everything beyond the feature.
- **Open seam:** the closed loop, the corpus, the OS thesis, the autonomy trust.

**How we co-exist and win against the startup wave:** They are feature-point competitors; we are the *loop the features plug into*. Our Ecosystem layer (agent SDK + plugin marketplace, Horizon 3) turns them from competitors into integrators — they can plug their feature into our loop if they emit interventions into our shared coordinate space, and our central scorer scores them. This is the OS-thesis commercial realization: competitors-as-plugins.

## 4. The existential threat — the AI surfaces themselves

OpenAI, Google/DeepMind, Anthropic, Perplexity, and the platforms (Meta, Apple) *could* ship a brand-authoring portal — a self-serve surface where a brand declares its facts and the surface considers them when generating answers. This would commoditize the Brand-Truth layer at the source.

**Why it's a real threat:** the surfaces have the incentive (reduce hallucination about brands), the distribution (every brand would register), and the trust signal (they ARE the answer-giver). Google's "Knowledge Graph" + Business Profiles are a partial precedent.

**Why it is not an immediate extinction event:**
- **Surfaces are single-surface; visibility is multi-surface.** A brand registers once and wants to be consistent across ChatGPT *and* Gemini *and* Perplexity *and* Grok. A surface-by-surface portal doesn't compose. The cross-surface aligned coordinate system is Engenox's structural advantage.
- **Surfaces do not have the causal corpus.** A portal declares facts; it does not learn which *interventions* lift *which outcomes* for *which brands* — and surfaces have no commercial reason to expose that learning (it would reveal their ranking mechanics). The calibrated learner is a third-party asset, not a surface-native one.
- **Surfaces have conflicting incentives.** A surface selling ads has no incentive to help brands "game" organic answers; the neutral third party has a cleaner trust posture.
- **The Brand-Truth primary-source reversal is bidirectional.** If enough brands consolidate their source-of-truth in Engenox, the surfaces have a *positive* incentive to integrate Engenox as the verified authority (fewer hallucinations, free-of-charge fact feed) rather than build their own portal. This is the 5–7-year north star in the Vision.

**How we defend:** the corpus + the aligned coordinate system + the cross-surface position + the calibrated learner. A portal commoditizes declaration; it does not commoditize measurement, learning, or action.

## 6. The two competitive invariants that survive every scenario

1. **No competitor or surface can purchase or backfill the consented closed-loop corpus on an aligned coordinate system.** It is path-dependent on consent + time. This is inviolable and is the floor of the moat — see `00_FOUNDATION_INTELLIGENCE_CORE.md` §3.
2. **Honesty calibrated-vs-overconfident is a positional choice, not a feature gap.** The funded leader chose overconfident-autonomy; we choose trust-first-autonomy. Neither can copy the other without re-architecting their causal story. As long as we *hold* the honesty position (CIs, coverage diagnostics, degradation alerts, "probe plan not guess," demote-on-alert), we own a defensible positional slot the overconfident entrants structurally vacate.

## 7. What this research tells us to do (and not do)

**Do:**
- Treat Profound as the funded ceiling; never claim parity on measurement polish — claim differentiation on repo-native + honest-CI + corpus.
- Treat the AI-vis startup wave as *future integrators* (the Ecosystem layer turn them into plugins), not head-on competitors.
- Treat the SEO incumbents as a *distribution* threat to be neutralized by the wedge verb + the published Index, not a *product* threat to be matched feature-for-feature.
- Treat the AI surfaces as the long-game existential threat that the cross-surface + corpus + calibrated-learner posture defends — and accelerate the corpus clock so the defense banks before they ship a portal.

**Do NOT:**
- Compete on measurement-polish (we lose to the funded unicorn).
- Compete on account-base breadth (we lose to the incumbents).
- Compete on feature-count (we lose to the startup wave's scattershot).
- Compete on "autonomous" as a marketing claim (we lose to the overconfident entrants and we *should* — trust-first is our slot).

We compete on the *result with honest proof, shipped into your repo, that compounds a corpus no one else can build*. That is the only ground we win, and it is the ground the moat is built on.
